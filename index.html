Cuts through web vulnerabilities.

SUDARSHAN crawls, attacks, and reports. A modular DAST engine that discovers pages and forms, fires rule-driven payloads at every input, and hands back a finding you can act on — no noise, no guesswork.

Three modules, one pass

Each stage does one job and hands off cleanly to the next — swap or extend any of them without touching the others.

01 — CRAWLER

Discovers pages & forms

Walks the target site, mapping every reachable page and input surface before a single payload fires.

02 — ENGINE

Fires payloads on every input

Runs each rule from dast_rules.json against every URL and form field it found.

03 — REPORTER

Writes JSON & HTML reports

Turns raw findings into scan_report.json and a readable HTML report you can hand off.
